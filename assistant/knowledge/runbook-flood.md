# Runbook: Flood level high (rule flood-high)

## Immediate actions
1. Confirm the reading against neighbouring flood sensors within 500 m. A single sensor spike with neighbours normal is usually a sensor fault.
2. Point the nearest PTZ cameras to preset `flood` and verify water on the road visually.
3. If water is confirmed, notify duty officers (SMS + push) and the civil defence liaison.

## Likely causes
- Heavy rainfall with storm drain blockage.
- Burst water main (check water network pressure drops nearby).
- Sensor fouling or calibration drift (flat-lined or erratic values).

## Escalation
- Level > 0.3 m on a carriageway: request road closure through the traffic management centre.
- Do not shelve flood alarms for more than 60 minutes without supervisor approval.
